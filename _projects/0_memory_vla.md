During my summer internship at MagicLab, I contributed to the early exploration of how memory could support vision-language-action policies over extended manipulation tasks.

### What I worked on

- Surveyed memory mechanisms for VLA models and long-horizon robotic manipulation.
- Helped set up training and evaluation workflows.
- Participated in a subset of model-training and testing experiments.
- Analyzed early memory-related behavior and failure modes across extended interactions.

### Contribution boundary

My contribution was limited to the project's early-stage literature review, experimental setup, and selected training and evaluation runs. I left before the final **Dual-Stream Memory** method was developed, so I do not claim the final architecture or its complete results as my work.

The figure above is from a later manuscript produced by the broader project and is included to show where that research direction ultimately led.
