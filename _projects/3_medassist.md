I helped build a retrieval-augmented pipeline that combines external medical knowledge with structured EHR tables through an LLM agent, retriever, and SQL or tool executors. I also worked on knowledge-graph construction and evaluation metrics that balance coverage and stability.

### What I worked on

- Connected external medical literature with structured EHR tables through retrieval and SQL or tool execution.
- Converted medical literature into structured knowledge graphs used by the agent pipeline.
- Designed pair, direction, and type variance metrics to study the trade-off between coverage and stability across runs.

The project was accepted to the **ACM Web Conference 2025 Demo Track**.
