BioMedJImpact

A large-scale dataset and LLM pipeline for biomedical journal-impact analysis.

BioMedJImpact combines 1.74 million PMC articles from 2,744 biomedical journals with citation, collaboration, and language-model-derived AI engagement signals.

What I worked on

  • Constructed and released the article-level dataset and journal-level analysis pipeline.
  • Designed a three-stage Gemma-based workflow to identify AI-related research and assign AI subfields.
  • Validated model outputs through human evaluation and analyzed relationships among collaboration, AI engagement, and scientific impact.
